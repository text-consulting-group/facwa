description_status: "This footage shows hospital and school construction in Paju Cheonhyeon-myeon and Beomwon-eup as part of the AFAK (Assistance to the Korean Armed Forces) program in March 1954. Notably, the greeting scenes where a U.S. forces official expresses gratitude for the construction shortly after the completion of Geumchon Civil Hospital in 1954 are very impressive. Scenes of students moving roof tiles for the Cheonhyeon National School construction and ongoing construction work follow. Additionally, construction scenes at a school located in Beomwon-ri are included. This is a meaningful video showing residents rebuilding schools destroyed by the war. In 1963, additional school and hospital expansions were made. [NEEDS REVIEW]"
