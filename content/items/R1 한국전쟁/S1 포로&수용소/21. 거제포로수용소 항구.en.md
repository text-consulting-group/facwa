description_status: "This footage shows the unloading of supply cargo arriving at the Geoje POW camp harbor and the disembarkation of prisoners of war. The United Nations Command operated two POW camps holding over 170,000 prisoners in Busan and Geoje. The Geoje camp alone held over 150,000 prisoners of war on a massive scale, requiring regular distribution of clothing, cookware, footwear, and other supplies. The camp command was responsible for providing three meals daily to the prisoners, receiving monthly shipments of grain and provisions. When large U.S. forces vessels approached the Geoje camp, amphibious vehicles transported the cargo to Goheun camp harbor. Prisoners of war were conscripted for the unloading work."
