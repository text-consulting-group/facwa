description_status: "This footage shows the re-education process and forced labor of prisoners of war. Pottery making was part of the re-education program rather than daily camp routine. Prisoners of war underwent ideological re-education through vocational training, hobbies, and artistic activities. The arts program encompassed music, fine arts, theater, dance, pottery, and other disciplines."
