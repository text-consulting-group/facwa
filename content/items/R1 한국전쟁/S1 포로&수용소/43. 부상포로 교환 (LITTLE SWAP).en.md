description_status: "This footage shows the process from April 24, 1953, of wounded North Korean People's Army prisoners of war being repatriated on a train from Busan through Daegu to Munsan Station, and the prisoner of war reception center established at the Freedom Village. Wounded prisoner of war exchanges began on April 20, 1953, with daily trains departing from Busan, passing through Daegu, and heading to Yeongdeungpo and Munsan stations. Scenes of wounded prisoners resting on the train and viewing the surrounding landscape are particularly noteworthy. They stopped briefly at Daegu Station before proceeding through Yeongdeungpo Station to Munsan Station. The footage shows the journey from Munsan Station to Daeseongdong Freedom Village and captures important exchange points including the reception headquarters and surrounding facilities. Additionally, the process of wounded prisoners arriving at Busan Port from Geoje or Jeju Island appears at the end. This footage shows unedited content. [NEEDS REVIEW]"
