description_status: "This footage shows wounded prisoners of war disembarking at Busan Port on April 17, 1953. LST 1096 at Busan Port is disembarking wounded prisoners of war. The prisoners were transported by ambulance to Busan Prisoner of War Camp No. 2 and Hospital Camp for temporary processing. They departed from Busan and arrived via Daegu-Daejeon-Yeongdeungpo. At the Yeongdeungpo Temporary Camp, wounded prisoners received further examination before departing for Munsan Station. After arriving at Munsan Station, the wounded prisoners moved to Freedom House and from April 20 to May 3, returned either north or south. [NEEDS REVIEW]"
