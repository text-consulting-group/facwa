title: "Wounded Prisoner Exchange Operation (Munsan Station and Freedom Village)"
description_status: "This footage shows the wounded prisoner exchange operation from April 19 to 20, tracing the route from Busan, Daegu, Munsan Station, Panmunjom, to Daeseongdong Freedom Village. The footage focuses primarily on Munsan Station and Daeseongdong Freedom Village. United Nations Command General Mark Clark personally inspects the movement process of wounded North Korean prisoners of war. Other senior commanders present include Eighth Army General Maxwell Taylor, I Corps Commander Paul Wilkins Kendall, and Far East Air Forces Commander Weyland, all observing the wounded POW repatriation operation. The footage contains the hospital base installed at Daeseongdong Freedom Village and its surrounding scenery. Clark and his party are shown watching returning allied prisoners of war from North Korea and exchanging handshakes. These officials had signed the Armistice Agreement at the UN Temporary Commission building in Seonnyeri on the morning of July 27, 1953, and then moved to Panmunjom."
