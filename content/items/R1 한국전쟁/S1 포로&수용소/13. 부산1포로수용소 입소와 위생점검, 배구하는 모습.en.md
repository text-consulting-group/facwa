title: "Admission, Sanitary Inspection, and Volleyball at Busan No. 1 Prisoner-of-War Camp"
description_status: "This footage shows prisoners undergoing a second round of registration at the Busan POW camp after capture. Immediately after capture, prisoners first completed intake at collection points, were transferred to temporary camps, interrogated, and then moved to formal camps. At the Busan POW camp, however, they were registered again. After registration, they received not only clothing but also rice bowls, soup bowls, cups, toothbrushes, toothpaste, and shoes. The borrowed uniforms were marked in red with 'PW' on the front, back, and trousers. Upon entering the camp, prisoners were sprayed with DDT from head to toe, then had their hair cut before finally receiving a late meal. The handling of prisoners in the camp followed U.S. Army Military Police field manuals FM 19-5 and FM 19-10, revised on September 14, 1950. FM 19-10 in particular laid out procedures from capture through confinement for prisoners and civilian detainees. A later manual, FM 19-40 on prisoner-of-war processing, appeared on May 1, 1952. This film is notable for documenting registration, physical inspection, camp organization, and food distribution according to the April 8, 1947 Military Police operations manual, FM 19-10."
