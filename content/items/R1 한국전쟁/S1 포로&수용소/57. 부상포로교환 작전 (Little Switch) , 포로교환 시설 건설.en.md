title: "Wounded Prisoner Exchange Operation (Little Switch) / Prisoner Exchange Facility Construction"
description_status: "This footage is one of several videos related to the Little Switch operation, the wounded prisoner exchange at Panmunjom. Unlike other Little Switch footage filmed primarily after April 20, this roll was filmed on April 10-11 and notably captures the construction work by engineering units building the various facilities at the Panmunjom POW exchange site."
description: "Shot list"
shotlist: "1. Shot list # Credit Roll 1 (0 min 0 sec, April 11 filming). Close-up of an engineering unit operating a bulldozer. The bulldozer levels ground to prepare the road and tent barracks site for the Little Switch operation. # Credit Roll 2 (1 min 03 sec, April 11 filming). Continuation of the previous scene. Lt. Col. William C. Poole of the 14th Engineer Construction Battalion points to the construction site and gives instructions to an officer beside him. Two personnel walk past the earthmoving site, conversing. # Credit Roll 3 (2 min 07 sec, April 11 filming). A scraper levels ground, raising dust. The scene shifts to Communist officers emerging from the tent where negotiations were held. Adm. John C. Daniels then appears, making a statement to reporters in an interview. # Credit Roll 1 (3 min 06 sec, April 11 filming). Aerial footage from a helicopter. Flying over the Munsan-ri area, the camera captures the press train and other rail cars stopped at the station. The scene shifts to an aerial view of the 45th Surgical Hospital at Freedom Village, then to the negotiation site at Panmunjom — the venue is empty, with two North Korean soldiers tidying the table. # Credit Roll 2 (5 min 04 sec, April 11 filming). Inside the negotiation venue. A North Korean flag and a United Nations Command flag rest on the table. A U.S. forces staff member explains the seating arrangement for the signatories, demonstrating by sitting down and simulating the signing. He then holds up the UN flag and the North Korean flag in turn for the cameraman. # Credit Roll 7 (6 min 06 sec, April 11 filming). After signing the agreement, Adm. Daniels steps outside the tent and explains the terms to reporters, holding a copy of the agreement. He engages in a question-and-answer session with reporters with a cheerful expression. # Credit Roll 1 (7 min 12 sec, starting at 10 sec of the second file, April 10 filming). Footage filmed one day earlier than the previous roll. Titled Peace Talks. An H-5 helicopter lands at Munsan-ri. Adm. Daniels descends and walks along the path. Several officers follow him, and the admiral and his party enter his tent in Munsan-ri."
