title: "Geoje POW Camp 64th Field Hospital / Suwol District / Fishing in Nearby Sea"
description_status: "This footage contains scenes of daily camp life, the 64th Field Hospital, the Suwol district camp surroundings, and the sea near Geoje. Under the Geneva Convention, prisoners of war were permitted to perform a certain amount of labor, though officers were exempt. Scenes of POWs marching to forced labor in the Suwol district appear briefly, filmed between March 5 and 7, 1952, before the camp was relocated. At the 64th Field Hospital established in the Suwol district, Korean nurses and U.S. forces medical officers and doctors treat prisoners of war. The hospital, situated between present-day Suwol and Yangjeong villages, was equipped with separate facilities including a hospital ward, surgical ward, and recovery room, and offered multiple departments — internal medicine, surgery, dentistry, and obstetrics — along with an inpatient ward. The footage also shows Geoje residents fishing in the waters near Haeseong, Jangmok, Neungpo-dong, and Yangji-am, in addition to Goheon where the Geoje POW camp was located. Traditional wooden fishing boats (tonggumi) used by Geoje fishermen around March 1952 are also visible."
