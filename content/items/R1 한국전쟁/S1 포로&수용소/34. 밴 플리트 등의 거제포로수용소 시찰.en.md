description_status: "This footage shows the visit of British Field Marshal Harold Alexander (1st Earl Alexander of Tunis, former Governor General of Canada) to Geoje Prisoner of War Camp with Van Fleet on June 16, 1952. Britain, along with Canada, Australia, and New Zealand, organized the 1st Commonwealth Division and participated in the Korean War, dispatching one company to the suppression operation at Geoje Camp Barracks 76 on May 27, 1952. After the kidnapping of Camp Director Dodd on May 7, Commonwealth forces were deployed for reclassification and relocation of prisoners of war, and they faced the dishonor of violent suppression during the Barracks 76 relocation process. The footage contains many scenes of Alexander, Van Fleet, and Camp Director Bortner inspecting the camp. [NEEDS REVIEW]"
