description_status: "This footage records everyday scenes and different camp sections at the Second Busan (Seomyeon) Prisoner-of-War Camp. The Busan POW camp selected a site within the Busan Logistics Base Command on July 15, 1950, began construction on July 18, and officially opened on July 30 with 39 prisoners. After the filming date, the Busan camp held 139,724 prisoners in total; 53,588 had already been transferred to Geoje Island on February 4, leaving 86,136 in Busan. After opening, the Busan camp expanded with branch camps at Yeongdo Haedong Middle School on August 1, Dongnae on August 18, Suyeong on November 9, and Gaya on December 8, while the Yeongdo branch was consolidated on August 13. On February 28, 1951, the Busan POW camp was reorganized into Areas 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 10, while the camps on Geoje Island became Areas 6, 7, 8, and 9 under POW Camp No. 1. The Busan camp and 'Camp No. 3' seen in this film correspond to Area 2 and Area 3."
