title: "Processing Prisoner Mail at the Geoje Prisoner-of-War Camp"
description_status: "This footage shows the prisoner-mail handling process at the Geoje prisoner-of-war camp on February 4, 1952. Under the Geneva Convention, prisoners were to be allowed to send letters to family members and others. The film begins with prisoners receiving letters. It then continues through scenes of a prisoner writing a letter, placing it into a mailbox, U.S. personnel collecting the letters, and Korean civilian workers censoring the correspondence. The film was made to show that the exchange of prisoner correspondence was being properly observed in accordance with the Geneva Convention. The prisoners shown in the footage appear neat and orderly, and other prisoners are shown putting letters into the mailbox."
