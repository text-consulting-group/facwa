description_status: "This footage shows the transport of wartime prisoners of war, life in the prisoner of war camp, and a portion of the reeducation program conducted by the Civil Information and Education Division, covering three main topics. In the first section (from 01 second to 2 minutes 12 seconds of Reels 30 and 32), prisoners of war are seen disembarking from amphibious landing craft (LSTs) at the pier of Geoje Prisoner of War Camp No. 1. From January 1951, the Geoje camp site selection, compound construction, and barracks building followed, with U.S. and ROK forces guard barracks accommodations being prepared. From March of the same year, prisoners of war were transported from Busan Prisoner of War Camp. As of the filming date of August 5, large-scale prisoner of war transport was still ongoing. The footage shows the camp exterior and interior from the pier to the camp headquarters and barracks. The second section contains scenes of prisoner of war camp life. One prisoner of war is cutting a 3-gallon (11.35-liter) drum to use as a washboard. In the camp, prisoners of war used various improvised tools that were difficult to obtain directly. Since camp headquarters could not provide washboards to prisoners of war, the U.S. forces cameraman appeared particularly interested in filming the prisoner of war washing clothes using a drum. The third section shows reeducation scenes conducted by the Civil Information and Education Division. Following the washing scenes, prisoners of war are seen engaging in volleyball, boxing, weightlifting, and various sports on the athletic field, as well as tent church activities, Barracks 83 school, model ship building, and other vocational education, ideological reeducation, and religious activities.
