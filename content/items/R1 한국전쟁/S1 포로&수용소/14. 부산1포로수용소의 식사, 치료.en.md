description_status: "This footage shows prisoners at the Busan POW camp receiving meals and medical treatment on February 22, 1951. The opening scenes follow food distribution at Busan No. 1 Prisoner-of-War Camp, at a time when prisoners were gradually being transferred to Geoje Island. After receiving rations, prisoners sit and eat nearby. Later scenes show the hospital compound at the Busan camp and surgery taking place, apparently on a prisoner suffering from frostbite, at the 14th Field Hospital. Doctors, medical assistants, and nurses are seen treating patients. The film also records an International Committee of the Red Cross visitor speaking with prisoners. In the early stage of the war, ICRC personnel visited UN-run camps to inspect management and sanitary conditions and reported their findings to headquarters in Switzerland."
