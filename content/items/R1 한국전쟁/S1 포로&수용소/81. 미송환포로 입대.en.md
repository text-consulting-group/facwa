title: "Non-repatriated Prisoners of War Enlistment"
description_status: "This footage shows scenes of non-repatriated prisoner of war released from Panmunjom on January 21, 1954, entering the 3rd Pohang Recruit Training Center. Among these prisoner of war are those who escaped on June 18, 1953, under Syngman Rhee orders. The ROK forces enlisted so-called Anti-Communist Patriot Youth prisoner of war into the military to serve in the military or police stations. Former prisoner of war were relocated to Gunsan, Pohang, and Seogwipo in Jeju Island for military training and ideological re-education. The footage shows large-scale welcome events from Pohang residents and enlistment ceremonies for former prisoner of war. Former prisoner of war underwent two rounds of ideological re-screening and education at the Army Counterintelligence Corps and Naval Counterintelligence Corps. [NEEDS REVIEW]"
