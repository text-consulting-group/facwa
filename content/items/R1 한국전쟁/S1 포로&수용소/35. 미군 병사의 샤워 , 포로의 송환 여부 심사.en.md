description_status: "This footage shows scenes of determining repatriation status from June 23 to 24, 1952. The United Nations Command headquarters conducted camp reprocessing based on the repatriation status of prisoners of war detained in Busan and Geoje from April to June 24, 1952. Those opting for repatriation to North Korea or China were relocated to Geoje, Geoje Jeoguri, or near Jeju International Airport. Those refusing repatriation from the North Korean People's Army and Chinese People's Volunteers who chose to go to Taiwan were relocated to various locations including Gaya and Seomyeon in Busan, Masan, Gwangju Sangmu-dae, Yeongcheon, Daegu, Nonsan, Incheon Bupyeong, and Mosulpo on Jeju Island. In the footage, prisoners of war are seen entering partitioned outdoor areas and responding to whether they choose repatriation or non-repatriation under U.S. forces instructions. However, during the repatriation screening process, intense conflicts including various demonstrations and confrontations occurred within the camp. [NEEDS REVIEW]"
