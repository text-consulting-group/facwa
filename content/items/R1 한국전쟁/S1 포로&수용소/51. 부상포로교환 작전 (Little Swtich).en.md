description_status: "This is one of several reels depicting scenes related to the Little Switch operation, the wounded prisoner exchange at Panmunjom. Compared with other reels, this footage is distinctive for capturing various views of Freedom Village at Munsan-ri and the 45th Surgical Hospital."
description: "Shot list"
shotlist: "1. Shot list # Credit Roll 5 (0min 0sec, filmed April 20). Prisoners of war being transported at Freedom Village in Munsan-ri. United Nations Command prisoners of war are shown being transported in Communist-side ambulances. Admiral Daniels and other officers watch the scene. The wounded are unloaded one by one from the Communist ambulances, including soldiers on stretchers. They are then transferred in turn to U.S. forces ambulances, which depart in succession carrying the prisoners of war. # Credit Roll 1 (2min 08sec, filmed April 20). An early morning scene filmed at Freedom Village, Munsan-ri. The 45th Surgical Hospital is visible. A UN flag flies. A large sign reading 'Welcome! Gate to Freedom' hangs in front of what appears to be a hospital tent. # Credit Roll 2 (3min 06sec, filmed April 20). Signs reading 'Freedom Village' are posted in front of the hospital tents. The 'Welcome! Gate to Freedom' sign is shown again from a different angle. The scene cuts to four soldiers chatting, drinking coffee, and smoking in a casual moment in front of a tent. # Credit Roll 3 (4min 05sec, filmed April 20). Continuation of the previous scene, the four soldiers drinking coffee, smoking, and chatting. A close-up shows one of them looking at his watch, which reads 6:50 AM. # Credit Roll 4 (5min 07sec, filmed April 20). A group of U.S. forces soldiers stand waiting in front of tents in the early morning. The camera then briefly shows the U.S. Army 45th Surgical Hospital. The scene moves inside a tent, the processing tent built for receiving prisoners of war, which is still empty. The prisoners will soon arrive. Cots are already prepared inside, with bedding neatly arranged. Pajamas for the prisoners are also neatly laid out beside the cots. # Credit Roll 5 (6min 06sec, filmed April 20). A close-up of medical supplies on a table. Surgical instruments are organized in a storage case. Personal locker contents are also visible. These are scenes showing the hospital's interior supplies. The scene cuts to the 'Welcome! Gate to Freedom' sign again, then zooms in on a 'Medical Pros' sign. # Credit Roll 6 (7min 01sec, filmed April 20). A medic stands beside a wooden storage box containing surgical instruments, bandages, stethoscopes, and other items. A close-up follows showing reports (AGC Report) and supply boxes. # Credit Roll 6 (8min 03sec, filmed April 20). General Paik Sun-yup is shown. He is seen speaking with other officers whose identities are difficult to determine precisely. The scene then shows generals, soldiers, and newspaper reporters standing near the operation area, conversing among themselves. They are waiting for the prisoners of war to arrive."
