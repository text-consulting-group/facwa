title: "Unrepatriated Prisoners of War Departing for Panmunjom for Release (North Korean Peoples Army, Chinese Peoples Volunteers)"
description_status: "This footage shows the release of unrepatriated Chinese People's Volunteers and North Korean People's Army prisoners of war departing for Panmunjom, filmed on January 20, 1954, by the 1st Marine Division Signal unit. Of the approximately 14,000 Chinese People's Volunteers prisoners of war in total, those held at Mosulpo on Jeju Island were temporarily housed at the Incheon Bupang No. 10 prisoner of war camp before being transported via Freedom Bridge to Panmunjom. They departed that same day from Incheon Port to Keelung Port in Taiwan. Some prisoners of war were flown to Taiwan by aircraft. Additionally, North Korean People's Army prisoners of war who had been unable to escape following President Syngman Rhee's directive of June 18, 1953, were released at Panmunjom and transferred to the Pohang 2nd Army Recruit Training Center for military and re-education programs."
