description_status: "This footage shows the surroundings of the Armistice Agreement signing hall at Panmunjom (now the Peace Museum) in North Korea on July 27, 1953, as well as a pre-signing ceremony held at 1:00 PM that afternoon at the United Nations Command Advance Headquarters in Seonyuri, where UN Command officials signed in advance and held a press conference. Typically, footage of the Panmunjom signing hall is edited together with the advance signing at the UN Command headquarters for broadcast media. This footage is notable because it shows the Panmunjom and Seonyuri signing and press conference as distinct events. The UNKRA Film Unit referenced in the title was established on December 1, 1950, under a UN resolution as part of the United Nations Korean Reconstruction Agency (UNKRA), and primarily filmed relief activities in Korea for the UN Public Information Department. The 304th Signal Operations Battalion was a well-known unit that operated under the U.S. Eighth Army Command headquartered at Yongsan Base, participating in prisoner of war exchange operations and remaining active through the postwar period into the 1970s."
