description_status: "This footage depicts the armistice negotiations held consecutively at Kaesong immediately following the commencement of the main talks on July 10, 1951. Multiple recordings from the same period and location exist and are documented in other record groups as well. The footage provides a relatively detailed view of both delegations and their surroundings right after the main talks began. The 167th Signal Photographic Company was a filming unit directly attached to the Far East Command, and this was shot by White. Overall, the footage is stable with well-composed framing and scene arrangement. The camera used was an Eyemo 35mm, a widely popular model at the time. This material holds considerable potential for use in the <Panmunjom & DMZ> exhibition and archive proposed by the research team."
