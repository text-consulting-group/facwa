description_status: "This footage shows the release of three ROK forces personnel captured by North Korean forces on September 13, 1955. The three Korean fishermen featured in the footage were identified by the press as ROK forces on September 15, 1953 (Dong-A Ilbo, 1955.9.15). On September 13, North Korea announced it would release three ROK forces personnel captured as spies. At a joint military ceasefire committee secretariat meeting between the United Nations Command and North Korea, North Korea stated it would hand over the three ROK forces personnel to the UN side at Panmunjom on the 17th. The United Nations Command denied the North Korean allegation that the three ROK forces personnel had been dispatched as armed spies to North Korea. North Korea claimed that the three ROK forces personnel had dispatched two armed spies into North Korean waters on July 22 to kidnap a number of North Korean fishermen for espionage purposes. The footage description notes that 'three fishermen are being brought from North Korea at the 141st Military Armistice Commission meeting.' Though brief, the footage shows the early Panmunjom area and surroundings of the Neutral Nations Supervisory Commission."
