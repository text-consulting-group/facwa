description_status: "This footage shows then-Vice President Nixon visiting the Phelan Base at the United Nations Command Advance Headquarters in Seonyuri, Munsan-myeon, and the ROK 1st Division outpost area beginning November 14, 1953. The Phelan Base and DMZ are visible approximately four months after the Armistice Agreement was signed. Nixon served as the 36th Vice President from January 1953 to January 1961 and was elected the 37th President in January 1969. This footage is significant as it documents Nixon's first visit to Korea after being elected Vice President."
