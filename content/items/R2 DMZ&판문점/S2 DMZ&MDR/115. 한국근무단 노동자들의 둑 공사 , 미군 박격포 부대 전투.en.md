description_status: "This footage shows Korean Service Corps workers mobilized over September 27–28, 1951 to rebuild a flood-damaged bridge along the Imjin River. The destroyed bridge was the Honker Bridge, which had been repeatedly swept away by floods. Scenes of Corps soldiers personally using stones to restore the crossing are particularly striking. The footage was filmed by Perez of the 226th Signal Company, assigned to the Far East Command. The 226th Signal Company also filmed activities at a prisoner of war camp."
