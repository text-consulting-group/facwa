description_status: "This footage was compiled from two aerial filming sessions on September 30 and October 3, 1967, covering scenes from the Paju DMZ through wire fence construction to the Imjin River railway bridge. It particularly shows the process of the Demilitarized Zone (DMZ) being converted into a wire fence barrier. Additional aerial footage includes Panmunjom and the Joint Security Area (JSA), and forward guard posts. This is the first time the footage has been released to the public. The filming unit was the U.S. Army's Special Photographic Office (SPDO), with Wade of the same 4th Section handling the work. The office was established in 1962, organized into three zones — the continental United States, Panama, and the Pacific — and provided footage to the Department of Defense, the Joint Chiefs of Staff, and the U.S. Congress. This unit operated under unlimited authority by order of President John F. Kennedy and expanded during the Vietnam War."
