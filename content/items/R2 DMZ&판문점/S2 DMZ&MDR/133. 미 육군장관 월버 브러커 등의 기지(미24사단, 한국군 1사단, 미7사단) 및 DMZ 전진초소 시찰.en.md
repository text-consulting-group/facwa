description_status: "This footage contains U.S. Army Secretary Wilber Brucker and his entourage visiting the ROK 1st Division sector within Panmunjom in Paju's-gunnae-myeon and the 24th Division garrison. The 24th Division garrison is the current Pelham Base in Seongyuri, Munsan-eup, while the ROK 1st Division post is a guard post near Josan-ri in Gunnae-myeon. The footage shows the Pelham Base after it was transferred from the U.S. Marines 1st Division to the 24th Division. It is particularly valuable for understanding the early status and condition of DMZ guard posts in the Paju area."
