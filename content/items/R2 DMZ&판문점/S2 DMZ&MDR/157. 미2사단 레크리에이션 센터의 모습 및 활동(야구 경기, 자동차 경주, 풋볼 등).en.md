description_status: "This footage shows the interior and exterior of the U.S. 2nd Infantry Division's 1st Recreation Center on July 26, 1970. The facility was used from 1955 to 1971 by the 24th Infantry Division, 1st Cavalry Division 7th Regiment, and 2nd Infantry Division in Yongjugol, Beobeon-ri, Beobeon-eup. Soldiers primarily visited on holidays to enjoy various games and other leisure activities. The filming unit was the Department of the Army Special Photographic Office (SPDP), with Wade from the same 4th Division assigned to the project. This unit was reorganized in 1962 into three geographic divisions covering the continental United States, Panama, and the Pacific, and provided footage to the Department of Defense, Joint Chiefs of Staff, and the U.S. Congress. Notably, this unit operated with broad authority under President John F. Kennedy's directive and expanded during the Vietnam War."
