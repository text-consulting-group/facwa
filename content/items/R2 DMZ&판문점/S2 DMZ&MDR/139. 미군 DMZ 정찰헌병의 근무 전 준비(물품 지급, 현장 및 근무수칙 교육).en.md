description_status: "This footage was filmed by the 125th Signal Photo Company at Paju DMZ in 1956. It shows DMZ area signage and various warning signs, similar to the earlier LC-44331 footage. The footage also displays posters detailing DMZ duty procedures and reporting methods. DMZ duty personnel were required to operate in three areas: civil administration, security, and intelligence. Of particular significance, the footage details individual DMZ duty regulations that cannot be verified through photographs or documents alone. The 125th Signal Company served with distinction from World War II through the Korean War and Vietnam War."
