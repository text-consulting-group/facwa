Document RG 127, UD-UP 19, Box 32, Command Diary report (March 1953), confirms that the 1st Marine Division and 1st Engineer Battalion were stationed in Seonyuri Donyuul. Therefore, this footage shows the early appearance of Camp Pelham, which was adjacent to the United Nations Command Provisional Headquarters. The filming unit is unknown, but the cameraman was Kirsling. Overall, the footage is stable with good composition and scene arrangement. The camera used was an Arriflex 35mm, a popular model of that era. The footage also includes content from the armistice talks held at Naebongjang, Kaesong."
