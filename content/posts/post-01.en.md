## Overview

*NARA II holds a large body of photographs and moving images produced by both military and civilian photographers and cameramen on the themes of division, the Cold War, and war in Korea, North Korea, and Asia.*

In the photographic record, U.S. military still photographers appear alongside photographers from major photojournalism magazines, and their works survive in large numbers across U.S. Army Signal Corps and USIS record groups. These images capture not only military subjects and wartime scenes but also everyday life and Cold War societies across Korea and Asia, including Japan, Taiwan, and the Philippines. The film holdings likewise include many anti-communist developmental and psychological-warfare productions made by U.S. film companies and military cameramen. NARA II also preserves photographs and films shot in North Korea and the Soviet sphere between liberation and the outbreak of the Korean War, many of them seized by U.S. forces during the occupation of North Korea. Some materials include documentaries and feature films produced in North Korea after liberation.

*This symposium investigates the related documentary records as well, in order to examine the production and management of these visual sources through the lens of ideological-psychological warfare and affect under division and the Cold War.*
